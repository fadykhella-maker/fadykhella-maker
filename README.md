**Head of Pre-Sales Engineering — North & Latin Americas @ AMD**

My team designs end-to-end AI, cloud, networking, cybersecurity, and high-performance data center architectures with hyperscalers and AI-native compute customers, and drives enablement of AMD's ROCm software stack — including CUDA-to-ROCm migration — for enterprise AI deployment.

23 years driving AI, compute, and networking strategy for hyperscalers, CSPs, and global enterprises — across the Americas, EMEA, and APAC — with prior leadership roles at **Celestica** (Field CTO / Director, Sales Systems Engineering — Connectivity & Cloud), **Extreme Networks** (Director of Product Management, SP Data Center & Cloud), **Intel** (WW Service Provider Sales Solutions Architecture Leader), and **Cisco Systems** (Global Services Provider Product Management, and multiple systems-engineering leadership roles across EMEA and APAC/ANZ).

This GitHub is where I turn that architecture work into hands-on builds: reference stacks, deployment scripts, and lab configs for the AI infrastructure I design in my day job.
